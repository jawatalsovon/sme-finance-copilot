## 2. What the facts imply (inferences)

1. **[Inference] This is the first FinTechathon edition that scores agents on a scripted test.** The 2022–2024 tracks were generic AI, blockchain and product management, and the 2025 AI notice described prototypes in broad terms. The 2026 wording (scripted tasks, simulated environment, injection and privilege tests, tiered permissions, automated testing) is new. Most teams will have built a nice chat UI; fewer will have built something that survives a script.
2. **[Inference] The odds are better than the 2025 funnel suggests.** In 2025 the AI track had 135 teams and 77 submissions for 10 finals slots (about 13% of submissions). The 2026 International Track is restricted to overseas students with at least 60% non-Chinese members, so it is likely smaller. I have no registration numbers for it. Do not rely on this.
3. **[Inference] Topic E is probably less crowded than Topics A and F** (personal finance, the on-chain wallet), because personal-finance assistants are the easiest to build. No evidence; treat as a guess.
4. **[Inference] The reconciliation wording on the site is a gift.** Our "Cash Truth" is exactly the word the organiser used, and it is measurable against a known answer key.
5. **[Inference] The 2025 champion was a full pipeline with an action at the end (live trading) and an explicit anti-hallucination layer.** The lesson is not "build a quant engine" but "show a whole loop that ends in an action, with a visible reliability mechanism".
6. **[Inference] Judges include financial-industry people and AI researchers.** The first group will ask "would a bank trust this?" (approvals, audit); the second "does it really work?" (benchmarks, failure analysis).

## 5. Risks to our own plan

- **Unknown test harness.** If the judges' scripted tasks expect a particular API or output format we cannot see, our own suite will not catch it. Mitigation: email the organiser (OFFICIAL_RULES §8) and keep the interface simple and documented.
- **Overbuilding.** Time is three days. The cut line above is the guard.
- **Overclaiming.** Synthetic data, illustrative products and a sandbox are all fine if labelled. Unlabelled, they could count against us in compliance review.
- **Chinese-language signals.** The open-class videos and Chinese coverage may hold test details we cannot read. One Chinese-reading teammate skimming them would be worth an hour.
